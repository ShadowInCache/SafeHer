## 1. What SafeHer is

A personal safety app that works with a smart glove and smart glasses. It can
raise an alarm to contacts you nominate, automatically or when you ask it to.

## 2. What SafeHer is not — read this part

**SafeHer is not an emergency service.** It does not contact the police, an
ambulance, or any official responder on your behalf. It notifies the people you
list. In an emergency, call your local emergency number — 112 in India.

**It cannot promise to detect anything.** Detection relies on machine-learning
models that are wrong sometimes, in both directions:

- it may **miss** a real emergency — a fall the model scores below its
  threshold, a weapon out of frame, words it mishears;
- it may **raise a false alarm** — a dropped bag, a shout at a football match.

**It depends on things outside its control**: your phone's battery, network
coverage, Bluetooth, the wearables' batteries, and whether the server is
reachable. Any of these failing can stop an alert being delivered.

**Do not rely on SafeHer as your only safety measure.**

## 4. Your emergency contacts

You are telling us to contact these people on your behalf, which means:

- you confirm you have their permission to give us their details;
- they may be contacted at any hour, including by mistake if a false alarm
  fires;
- they will learn your location when an alert is raised.

## 5. Acceptable use

Do not use SafeHer to raise alarms you know to be false, to harass anyone, to
monitor another person without their knowledge, or to break the law.

Deliberately triggering false alarms wastes the attention of people who would
otherwise help you, and may be an offence.

## 6. Evidence and recordings

SafeHer can record audio and capture images during an emergency. Recording laws
differ by place, and in some jurisdictions recording others without consent is
illegal. **You are responsible for knowing the law where you are.**
